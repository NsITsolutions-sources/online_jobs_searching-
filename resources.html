10 Resume Tips for Students

Quick wins to make your CV stand out, even with limited work experience.

Tailor Every Application

Never send the same CV to every job. Read the job description carefully and include the keywords and skills they request. ATS software scores CVs based on keyword matches before a human even views them.

Keep It to One Page

A single page serves as the proper length for students who have just graduated from their studies. The first screening of resumes by recruiters takes about seven seconds to complete during their initial review. Make each line essential for your document while you should eliminate all irrelevant details which do not serve any purpose.

Quantify Your Achievements

Don't just say "managed social media" — say "grew Instagram following by 40% in 3 months." Numbers make vague claims concrete and memorable. Even university project results can be quantified.

Use a Clean, Readable Font

Stick to professional fonts like Calibri, Arial, or Inter at 10–12pt. Avoid decorative fonts, coloured backgrounds, or fancy borders — these often break when CVs are processed by ATS software.

List Relevant Projects

If you're lacking professional experience, your university projects, hackathon entries, and personal GitHub repos are gold. Give each project a brief description, the technologies used, and the outcome.
