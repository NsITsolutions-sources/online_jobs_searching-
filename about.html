About UniJobs

Built by students, for students — connecting the next generation of talent with great opportunities.

We bridge the gap between university talent and great employers.

UniJobs was born in a university library during exam season — a group of eight final-year Computer Science students frustrated by how hard it was to find flexible, student-friendly jobs that actually fit around lecture timetables and coursework deadlines.

We built the platform we wish had existed when we were in first year. Every feature — from the flexible-hours filter to the application deadline countdown — was designed with the real constraints of student life in mind.

Today, UniJobs partners with over 380 companies ranging from innovative startups to established Sri Lankan enterprises, all of whom are committed to investing in young talent.
